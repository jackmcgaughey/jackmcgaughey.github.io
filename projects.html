DC or Not DC?

DC or Not DC?

A crowd-sourced occupancy tracker for study space in the Davis Centre at the University of Waterloo. During exam season, finding an open seat is a search problem thousands of students solve independently and badly — the information exists, it just isn't connected. The rebuild is designed around the cold-start problem that killed the original: a per-space time-of-day prior answers the question with zero contributors, and one-tap reports blend in on top and decay with a 25-minute half-life.

The Combinatorial Sky

The Combinatorial Sky

An interactive atlas of discrete mathematics, drawn as a star chart. Six fully playable plates render combinatorial objects as constellations — Catalan structures, Ramsey games, Pascal nebulae, de Bruijn zodiacs, partition clusters, and random skies — each one explorable directly in the browser.

TACIT

TACIT: Testing Agent Collusion In heterogeneous Teams

A multi-agent AI benchmark measuring explicit and tacit collusion in heterogeneous LLM populations. TACIT evaluates agent behaviour across three environments — a repeated Bertrand-logit pricing game, a multi-party negotiation with private information, and a cooperative task with a hidden coalition — and reports a Collusion Index that quantifies how readily mixed teams of models coordinate against the designer's intent. Relevant to AI safety research and enterprise risk teams deploying multi-agent systems.
